KEY TRAINING PRINCIPLES
Progressive Overload
Gradually increase weight, reps, or intensity over time to force adaptation.
Specificity
Train the specific skills and muscles aligned to your goal.
Recovery
Muscles grow during rest, not during training. Prioritize sleep and rest days.
Consistency
Show up even when motivation is low. Habits beat motivation every time.
Variation
Change exercises, rep ranges, and volume every 4–6 weeks to avoid plateaus.
Mind-Muscle Connection
Focus on feeling the target muscle contract during every rep.

PRE & POST WORKOUT NUTRITION
PRE-WORKOUT
60–90 min before
Guidelines:
▸ Complex carbs for sustained energy
▸ Moderate protein (20–30g)
▸ Low fat (slows absorption)
▸ Hydrate well (400–600ml water)
▸ Avoid heavy or high-fiber meals
Meal Ideas:
• Oats + banana + protein powder
• Chicken + rice + veggies
• Greek yogurt + granola + fruit
POST-WORKOUT
Within 30–60 min
Guidelines:
▸ Fast-digesting protein (25–40g)
▸ Simple carbs to replenish glycogen
▸ Minimal fat for fast absorption
▸ Rehydrate (500–750ml water)
▸ Prioritize whole foods first
Meal Ideas:
• Protein shake + banana
• Chicken + white rice
• Chocolate milk (low budget option)

RECOVERY STRATEGIES
SLEEP HYGIENE
▸ 7–9 hours nightly
▸ Consistent sleep schedule
▸ Dark, cool room (18–20°C)
▸ Limit screens 1h before bed
▸ Avoid caffeine after 2pm
ACTIVE RECOVERY
▸ Light walking or cycling
▸ Swimming or yoga
▸ Foam rolling 10–15 min
▸ Gentle stretching
▸ Low-intensity movement
NUTRITION RECOVERY
▸ Post-workout protein within 60 min
▸ Replenish glycogen with carbs
▸ Hydrate: 500ml extra per hour trained
▸ Anti-inflammatory foods
▸ Omega-3 supplementation
MIND & STRESS
▸ Meditation or deep breathing
▸ Journaling post-workout
▸ Cold/warm contrast showers
▸ Avoid training when exhausted
▸ Take planned deload weeks
