The science behind ketosis is rooted in the body’s natural response to starvation or low carbohydrate availability. When carbohydrate intake is

eects of ketones. Additionally, the thermogenic eect of protein, which is typically higher in ketogenic diets, can further enhance calorie
burning. Thus, ketosis not only facilitates fat loss but also helps in maintaining muscle mass during weight loss eorts.
Moreover, ketosis has been linked to numerous health benets beyond weight loss. It has been studied for its potential eects on conditions
